Editing an on-call schedule used to replace prior edits to on-call shifts, which meant any overrides your team had already set up (someone covering a vacation, a swap between two engineers, a temporary reassignment) were silently lost. Admins learned to avoid touching a schedule once overrides were in place, and engineers couldn't fully trust that coverage they'd arranged would actually hold. What's new A dedicated Overrides view.
